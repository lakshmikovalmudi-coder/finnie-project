# 401(k) vs IRA vs Roth

Source: https://www.investor.gov/additional-resources/retirement-toolkit

- **401(k)**: an employer-sponsored retirement account. Contributions come out of
  your paycheck. Many employers offer a **match** — extra money added when you
  contribute.
- **Traditional IRA**: an individual retirement account you open yourself.
  Contributions may be tax-deductible now; withdrawals in retirement are taxed.
- **Roth IRA / Roth 401(k)**: you contribute after-tax money now; qualified
  withdrawals in retirement are tax-free. Roth IRAs have income limits.

The core trade-off between traditional and Roth is **pay tax now or later**.
Contribution limits change yearly — check IRS.gov for current figures.

This is general education, not tax advice. A tax professional can apply these
rules to a specific situation.
