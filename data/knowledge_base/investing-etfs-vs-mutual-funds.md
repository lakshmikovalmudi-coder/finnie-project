# ETFs vs Mutual Funds

Source: https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/mutual-funds-and-exchange-traded-funds-etfs

Both ETFs (exchange-traded funds) and mutual funds pool money from many investors
to buy a basket of securities, giving instant diversification.

| | ETF | Mutual fund |
|---|---|---|
| How you buy | Trades on an exchange like a stock, all day | Bought from the fund company at end-of-day price (NAV) |
| Minimum | Price of one share (or a fraction) | Often $1,000–$3,000 |
| Costs | Usually low expense ratios; no loads | Varies; some charge sales loads |
| Tax efficiency | Generally more tax-efficient in taxable accounts | Can distribute capital gains |

Many index funds exist in both forms. For beginners, the expense ratio and what
the fund holds usually matter more than the wrapper.
